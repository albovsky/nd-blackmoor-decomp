## Deliberate divergences and known limits

- Uninitialised locals zero; word arguments compare by their low 16 bits; the host contract of FL-21 (flush the world before each `Fl18Host.call`, resync after) applies.
- 0x47ff0e: the original copies a caption string (the one 0x423259 returns when it is at most 0x9c40 characters, or the record's string at +0x7fa, whose length is unchecked) into a 0xcc-byte frame buffer below its locals, then writes a dword at the terminator. A string of more than 200 characters overwrites its own locals, saved registers and return address. The native port returns `FL22B_47FF0E_REJECT_LONG_STRING` (1) for that case: effects made before the copy stay made, 0x448d11 is not called and nothing after runs. This is an explicit native rejection, not original behaviour, and is not exercised by the differential test (the original cannot be compared there); the direct test covers 200 bytes (accepted, frame bytes compared) and 201 and 0x9c40 (rejected). The return value is otherwise 0 and is not an original value (the original leaves garbage in eax).
- 0x42ed54: sub-step 0 reads `words_5b5138[i]` and the record bytes at +0x225+i for i below a signed word (record +0x223) with no bound against the 16-entry table; the original reads other globals past it. The native port reads past its pointer too; the count is the caller's contract (fixtures keep it within 16). No rejection was added.
- 0x42ed54: the flag-table store index (signed word from the record, `flags_585801`) is not range-checked, as in the original.
- `records_5bb5e5`, `flags_585801`, `bytes_5b5128`, `words_5b5138` are host buffers addressed at original offsets; the record objects (addresses kept in the records) are read through `Fl18Host.resolve` each time they are used, as the original re-reads `[ebp-4]`-derived memory.

## Unverified branches

- 0x47ff0e: callees that change `self` (for example 0x480b34 changing `+0x13ce`) are covered by one direct test via a host hook but not by the differential test. Item indices beyond the object (a callee returning a large selection word) are the callee's contract and are not covered.
- Strings longer than 200 characters in 0x47ff0e (see above) and a count above 16 in 0x42ed54 are not compared against the original.
- The callers of both functions and the callees listed under hooks are not part of this slice. No independent review has been done.

### Deliberate divergences and known limits (0x4b64a9)

- The original divides by `[record +0x25]` (a word, sign-extended) with `idiv` in step 2 (when no item with its flag word set is due, after the GetTickCount call) and step 4 (0x4b6d82, after the tick); a zero word raises a divide error. The native port returns `FL22B_4B64A9_REJECT_DIVIDE_BY_ZERO` (1) at that point: effects made earlier (the item loop of step 2) stay made, nothing after the tick is stored. The differential test keeps the divisor non-zero (the emulated original would fault); the direct test checks the rejection. Otherwise the return value is 0 (the original leaves garbage in eax).
- Uninitialised: the 16-byte local `[ebp-0x1c]` is zero; the callee 0x4b6355 fills it on every call in both tests. The item count (`record +0x21`, a signed word) is not bounded: the original writes `self + 0x24/0x124 + 16*i` and `self + 0x232/0x254 + 2*i` for every i below it and the port does the same on the raw bytes (a count above 16 overwrites other fields of `self`; the direct test stays within 16, the differential uses 17 and negative counts too, with identical raw memory). The slot word `self+0x1c` indexes the table at 0x5d1591 (stride 0x4a) unchecked, and the flag-table index (signed word) likewise.
- Word arguments compare by their low 16 bits (0x4b6355 arguments 0..2, 0x4fb2e3, 0x42ba3a); the pushed upper halves of the original are leftover register contents.
- The 0x4c2790 call passes the record object's original address (`rec_va`), the strings 0x551b3c and 0x551b54 as numbers; the table entry text is copied from `string_551b24` with its NUL (`rep movsd` plus `rep movsb` equals one memcpy of length + 1).

### Unverified branches (0x4b64a9)

The port reads those fields again after each call as the original does, which is by inspection only.
- The out-of-bounds cases above (count above 16 in the direct test, slot word beyond the table, flag-table index beyond its slack) are not compared with the original.

## Review follow-up (FL-22 root)

- 0x47ff0e long caption (more than 200 characters): the port keeps the stores made before the copy (`[0x1344]`=0, `[0x1348]`=1, both item copies, the 0x47ed96 call, `[0x13ca]`=1 and the 0x13e7 deadline), skips the tail from 0x4808af (item+0x22/+0x20 not cleared, item not reloaded, 0x47e52f not called, word 0x1350 not set to -1) and returns `FL22B_47FF0E_REJECT_LONG_STRING`. Because `[0x13ca]` stays set, the gate at 0x47ff76 blocks the handler until something else clears it, and the stale 0x1350 is reused. The original overruns its frame here (undefined); an integration host must treat the return code as "caption dropped" and not call the handler again until it has cleared those fields.
- No bounds checks (as the original): a count above 16 at rec+0x21 in 0x4b64a9 writes `self+0x124+16*i` and `self+0x232+2*i` past the object, 0x42ed54 reads past `words_5b5138` and the record, and 0x463392/0x4b64a9 index the 0x4a-stride slot table with an unchecked signed word. Callers and loaders must guarantee the ranges.
- The batch b mutation runs (33, 56 and 80 mutants) were made with scratch scripts that are not committed; the evidence is the worker's report, and the independent reviewer re-ran 45 mutants of its own (all survivors equivalent). The direct binary was built and run under ASan/UBSan at -O1 and -O2 by the worker and by the reviewer; no sanitizer test file is committed for batch b.
