## Final sleep-race correction (2026-11-01, 20:58 PST)

The follow-up review rejected 4905bfdd: a Sleep(0)/GetExitCodeThread poll could retain the mutex while its worker was returning from platform sleep; a suspended sleeper could also claim an empty execution token. The final fix releases the mutex through a bounded condition wait when the current guest is the sole runnable guest and another is sleeping, then recomputes eligibility. A waking sleeper respects its suspend count, except during shutdown. Shutdown can wait for a platform sleep callback to return.

Nine thread cases plus four route-checker cases pass (13 total, 1.325 seconds). Both new race regressions fail by ordinary bounded CHECK exit 1 on the private frozen 4905bfdd baseline and pass with the fix; no operating-system crash or fault injection is involved.
